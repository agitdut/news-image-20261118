Entry for make.fun's `news-image-20261118` bounty ($250, program/v1): one
image summing up the world's news of Wednesday 18 November 2026.

The image is a dense single front page (`draw_news.py`, Pillow), with a
masthead naming the day, a Gulf lead story beside a world-at-a-glance map
and the Shenzhen summit, three illustrated features, three columns of
short items, a markets strip and an also-in-brief footer — about 27
stories in all, each reported on 18 November 2026:

1. **Iran's drones reach Kuwait and Bahrain** — Tehran widens its
   attacks after the U.S. strike on Qeshm Island; Bahrain intercepts
   attacks for a third time this week; a Houthi missile aimed at Riyadh
   is destroyed and the tanker Tara Gas is hit in Hormuz.
2. **Xi, Putin and Trump to meet in Shenzhen** — Xi opens the APEC
   summit and talks with Putin; Trump joins them on Thursday.
3. **Ukraine hits Kerch bridge a second day** — part of a road span
   falls into the strait; 2 killed.
4. **Ebola outbreak more than doubles** — 11,376 cases and 5,568
   deaths; nearly three in four infected children under 5 die.
5. **Nvidia doubles revenue; no Huang return** — quarterly revenue
   hits $115.8B; the board rules out Jensen Huang coming back as CEO.
6. **Democrats win Alaska's Senate seat** — Mary Peltola's victory
   gives the party 56 seats.
7. Short items: US loses measles-free status; NSA network outage;
   Asif Merchant sentenced; Waymo off US highways; Philippines trade
   deal; UK sanctions over West Bank settlements; Knesset elects Kahana;
   Houthis near Aden; Haiti protests; Mongolia's president resigns;
   Costa Rican journalist killed; Deutsche Bank leak; US AI-chip curbs;
   UK inflation 3.6%; France protests; Indigenous group emerges in the
   Amazon; South Korea's AI exam-cheating bounties — plus Wednesday's
   market closes (S&P 500 −1.03%, Brent $91.55, Bitcoin $64,649).

Sources: Reuters, AP, BBC, UN News, Le Monde, ECDC.

The program (`main.ts`) copies `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png`.
The file is a PNG under 1 MiB, 1024 by 1024, within the gate's limits.
