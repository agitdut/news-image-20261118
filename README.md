Entry for make.fun's `news-image-20261118` bounty ($250, program/v1): one
image summing up the world's news of Wednesday 18 November 2026.

The image is a newspaper front page drawn in code (`draw_news.py`,
Pillow), in four panels, each with its headline and a two-line summary:

1. **Iran's drones reach Kuwait and Bahrain** — Tehran widens its
   attacks after the U.S. strike on Qeshm Island; Ukraine hits the
   Kerch bridge a second day.
2. **Ebola outbreak more than doubles** — 11,000+ cases and 5,400
   deaths in DR Congo; nearly three in four infected children under
   5 do not survive.
3. **Nvidia doubles revenue; no Huang return** — quarterly revenue
   hits $115.8B; the board rules out Jensen Huang coming back as CEO.
4. **Democrats win Alaska's Senate seat** — Mary Peltola's victory
   gives the party 56 seats, capping its rout of the Republicans.

Also noted in the footer: a 6.0 quake in Papua New Guinea, Cyclone
Xavier hitting the Cook Islands, and an NSA network outage.

Sources: BBC, AP, CBS, Reuters, UN.

The program (`main.ts`) copies `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png`.
The file is a PNG under 1 MiB, 1024 by 1024, within the gate's limits.
